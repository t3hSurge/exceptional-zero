## Test candidate

> **Orientation is asymmetric assignment of relata to non-interchangeable positions within a relation.**

This is a positive structural characterization. Philosophical accounts of non-symmetric relations distinguish differential application, direction, and positional structure as ways in which the order of relata can be structurally significant. citeturn0search0

## Leak check 2 — transition, occurrence, or (O_2)

An asymmetric assignment of relata to positions does not entail:

- transition;
- occurrence;
- passage;
- succession;
- exercise.

For a binary relation, the difference between (R(a,b)) and (R(b,a)) can be represented by differential role assignment without any claim that one relatum gives way to another. Non-symmetric relations can therefore bestow order without constituting a transition. citeturn0search0turn0search3

The prior distinction survives.

**Pass.**

## Leak check 3 — mere descriptive restriction

The candidate is not merely:

- Aut-talk;
- completeness talk;
- internal-relations talk;
- or a restriction on which predicates are allowed in a description.

It specifies a structural condition: the relata occupy non-interchangeable relational positions.

A description may fail to mention those positions, but the candidate itself is not defined as a restriction on description.

This does not establish that every ontology must contain orientation, nor that the Cut derives orientation.

It establishes only that orientation itself has a positive structural specification independent of an atemporal foil.

**Pass.**
