# Act Upstream of Actuality

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Open — AP1 run; no upstream act specification earned  
**Depends on:** `2026-09-03-act-behind-structure.md` (complete at this level; not rewritten)  
**Not in scope:** AB8; treating a missing explanation as the proposed explanation; AT or verb-first as lemmas; $Z(S)$ as particularity; canonical edit; mover; time primitive; `G*_2`

The previous investigation tested, among other things, whether actuality requires act as an explanatory constituent.

That arrow was not earned. It may have been facing the wrong way.

A missing act-free account of actuality is **not** evidence that act is the missing ingredient.

---

## Reverse target

$$
\boxed{\text{Can an act be coherently specified without presupposing an independently given actuality?}}
$$

Austere form:

Can “act” be given metaphysical content such that structure is intelligible as a stabilized or frozen consequence of it, without first assuming the actuality of either the act or the resulting structure?

Intended direction under test, not assumed:

$$
\text{act}\;\rightarrow\;?\;\rightarrow\;\text{structure / actuality}
$$

rather than:

$$
\text{actual structure}\;\rightarrow\;\text{what explains actuality?}\;\rightarrow\;\text{act.}
$$

Beauty of the intuition does not set the arrow.

---

## What must not be smuggled

- An already-actual agent who acts.
- An already-actual structure that the act modifies.
- Occurrence, performance, or passage used as synonyms for the act under definition.
- Temporal succession as the content of “act.”
- “Act” as whatever is left when structure is subtracted from actuality.

---

## AP1 — First specification pressure

Target:

> Specify $\mathrm{Act}$ with no independently given $\mathrm{Actual}(-)$ in the primitive vocabulary.

The test is not whether the word *act* can be used. It is whether an act-like ontological distinction can be given content without importing actuality, execution, occurrence, or an equivalent target through another name.

### A. Relational act

Candidate:

$$
\operatorname{Act}(x,y).
$$

As a bare primitive relation, this is formally coherent. But its distinguishing content from an ordinary static relation is absent. Calling the relation an “act” does not make it active.

If additional content is supplied — exercised, effective, operative, productive, occurring — that content introduces the target by another route.

Earned bounded result:

$$
\boxed{\text{Bare relational form does not distinguish act from static relation.}}
$$

### B. Transformational act

Candidate:

$$
A:S\mapsto S'.
$$

The map is structurally clear and can encode a difference between configurations. But a represented transformation is not thereby an enacted transformation.

The model can contain the complete graph of the mapping without containing an execution of the mapping. Adding execution restores the very actuality/occurrence content excluded by the test.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{\text{formal transformation}\neq\text{actual act}}
$$

among the tested formulations.

### C. Dispositional act

Candidate: characterize act through power, capacity, or disposition.

This distinguishes a thing's being able to produce a change from the production itself:

$$
\text{capacity to act}\neq\text{acting}.
$$

The capacity can therefore be specified without an act-token. But that makes it a disposition or potency, not yet the act sought here.

If the act is recovered by saying that the capacity is exercised, realized, or actualized, the specification reintroduces actuality/performance content.

Earned bounded result:

$$
\boxed{\text{Disposition can be act-adjacent without being the act.}}
$$

### D. Primitive act

Allow $\operatorname{Act}$ as an irreducible primitive with no reduction to relation, transformation, or disposition.

This is not automatically incoherent. But two readings separate:

1. **Pure label:** “Act” is primitive because we stipulate that whatever is primitive and called Act is an act. No independent discriminating content is supplied.
2. **Primitive with content:** Act carries some additional content such as exercise, efficacy, occurrence, realization, or production. Those candidates import actuality/performance/occurrence or an equivalent semantic target.

No third content-bearing specification was isolated in this pass.

### E. Negative-space definition

Candidate:

$$
\operatorname{Act}:=\text{whatever remains after static structure and actuality are removed}.
$$

This is explicitly circular for the present investigation. It does not specify act upstream; it defines act as a residue of the very distinction under test.

### AP1 result

Across the tested forms:

$$
\boxed{\text{No tested non-circular specification of Act has been produced without importing actuality, occurrence, performance, or equivalent content.}}
$$

The result is a specification result, not a priority theorem.

In particular, AP1 does **not** establish:

- act is impossible;
- actuality necessarily precedes act;
- act is identical with actuality;
- act is identical with temporal occurrence;
- structure is caused by act.

The strongest surviving counterpressure is therefore still a primitive act vocabulary — but no independently discriminating content has yet been supplied for it.

A useful working distinction now appears:

$$
\boxed{\text{act as a formal operator}\neq\text{act as an actually exercised operation}}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{\text{act-adjacent capacity}\neq\text{act}}.
$$

### Next pressure

Do not open a generic AB8.

The next legitimate question is narrower:

> **Can “exercise” or “efficacy” itself be specified without presupposing actuality, occurrence, or performance?**

That question should be opened only as a new object if an independent reason to treat exercise/efficacy as the missing content emerges.
