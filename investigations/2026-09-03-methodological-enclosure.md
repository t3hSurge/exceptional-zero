## Locked so far

M1–M3 handled. M4 isolated. Evidence trilemma is a symptom, not a theorem. Meta-Boundary unadopted.

Candidate failure $\neq$ class failure $\neq$ model-space failure. Only the first two are events $A$ currently recognizes. “Model-space failure” names an unassigned remainder after the AT stall test. It is not a fired event.

$$
\boxed{A\text{ can recognize that its current candidates have failed.}}
$$

$$
\boxed{A\text{ has not yet been shown to recognize that it has stopped representing the thing under investigation.}}
$$

Those claims are asymmetric. Neither implies $A$ is inadequate.

Ladder:

$$
\text{candidate failure}\ \rightarrow\ \text{class failure}\ \rightarrow\ \text{model-space failure}\ \rightarrow\ \text{?}
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Do not fill the }?\text{.}}
$$

Two illegitimate ascents:

- “We found something $A$ cannot represent, therefore $A$ is inadequate.”
- “$A$ does not recognize model-space failure, therefore model-space failure is not real.”

Deeper-question isolation moves the question upstream. It still assumes the new question lives in the same event ontology.

## 17. Next pressure — representational self-audit

Do not fill the $?$. Do not construct $M'$ or $Q$. Do not add a sixth status.

$$
\boxed{
\text{Can a methodology detect a failure of its own representational level without presupposing a representational level capable of expressing that failure?}
}
$$

This is not a conclusion. It is the next pressure.

If the answer is yes, the detecting level must be specified independently of the failed level, without becoming a disguised restatement of $A$, and without smuggling an escape hatch.

If the answer is no among tested formulations, that is another stall: self-audit bottoms out. A stall of that kind would not yet tell us whether the bottom is an epistemic boundary or an unfinished method.

If the question cannot even be posed without already using a level that can express “representational failure,” the question exhibits the same shape it is asking about. Record that shape. Do not promote it.
