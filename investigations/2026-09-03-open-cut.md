## OC5 — Is the demand for a terminal boundary itself an illicit hidden Cut?

Question actually asked. Not assumed.

$H_{\mathrm{OC}}$ under the knife:

$$
H_{\mathrm{OC}}:\ \text{the demand for terminal closure converts a provisional stopping point into metaphysical closure.}
$$

Also under the knife:

$$
\text{The demand for a final boundary is itself the Cut.}
$$

### OC5-A. Logical necessity of terminal closure

No theorem was exhibited that inquiry-as-such must have a last boundary. “Otherwise contradiction” still owes the contradiction. None was produced in this pass from the bare refusal to declare a last inquiry.

### OC5-B. Semantic necessity

Is “open” incoherent without an endpoint?

Only if “open” is defined as “not-yet-arrived-at-its-last-point.” That builds the endpoint into the word. An open interval is open without a last point. Semantic necessity of an endpoint for “inquiry” was not isolated independently of that stipulation.

### OC5-C. Pragmatic necessity

Must inquiry function by means of a *final* boundary?

Functioning agents stop particular tasks. That is not yet a last task. No independent showing that a practice of inquiry is impossible unless some investigation is declared unreassessable.

### OC5-D. Epistemic necessity

Does revisability require a non-revisable rule?

The candidate “the rule that rules are revisable is itself not revisable” *is* an exemption. It does not derive the exemption; it installs one. No independent argument was isolated that a meta-rule cannot be held only at $t$.

### OC5-E. Regress / relocated Cut

Does refusing a terminal boundary merely relocate $Z$?

$Z(S)$ is non-transitivity of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ — a structural condition on particularity. A demand that inquiry end is a methodological act. Those are different categories.

Particular inquiries can exist (each a this) without there being a last inquiry. Particularity does not entail a last particular.

Calling every distinction “a Cut” inflates $Z$ into a nickname for binaries. That identification was not earned.

Non-Cut accounts of the *demand* for a last boundary exist as candidates and were not eliminated: institutional last-word tasks; confusion of $\mathrm{Complete}(I)$ with $\mathrm{Final}(\text{inquiry})$; preference for closed exposition. Their existence is enough to block “the demand *is* $Z$.”

### $H_{\mathrm{OC}}$

**Wide reading** (“any demand for terminal closure”): killed. “Close this case this year,” “prove $T$ in $S$” demand closure of a framed task. They do not convert inquiry-as-such into a finished metaphysics.

**Narrow reading** (“demand for terminal closure *of inquiry as such*”): nearly restates what those words mean. That is not a derived identification with the Cut, and it is not a theorem that every such demand is illicit.

### Cut-identification

Not established. Category mismatch (structural fact vs methodological demand) was not bridged. Metaphor is not a result.

### What would have confirmed the illicit-Cut thesis

A showing that the demand for a last inquiry *is* $Z$, or that every warrant for that demand is nothing but an exemption from reassessment *and* that this makes the demand illicit.

OC5 did not show the identity. It did not show illicitness as such.

It did show: in this pass, the warrants offered for terminal closure *of inquiry as such* were framed-task requirements, stipulations, or installed exemptions. That is a local attack result, not a use of OC1–OC4 as lemmas.
