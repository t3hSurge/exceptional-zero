## 7. OC1 — Is terminal closure necessary?

Question actually asked:

$$
\boxed{\text{What would make terminal closure necessary rather than merely useful?}}
$$

Sequence under test:

$$
\text{finite agent} \;\rightarrow\; \text{finite inquiry} \;\rightarrow\; \text{provisional stopping} \;\rightarrow\; \text{?}
$$

The `?` is whether any arrow to *terminal closure* is warranted.

$R$ is not used as a premise.

### 7.1 Candidate warrants

For each candidate $N$: does $N$ require **closure**, or only **stopping this inquiry**?

**Finite resources / finite agent.**  
A finite agent cannot keep every inquiry open. That licenses $\mathrm{Stop}(I)$ when attention or time runs out. It does not license $\mathrm{Final}(\text{inquiry})$. Countermodel: indefinitely many finite inquiries, each stopped, none globally final.

**Contradiction avoidance.**  
Not asserting both $P$ and $\neg P$ constrains what is *presently held*. It does not require that some $P$ be exempt from later reassessment. You can cease asserting without closing.

**Logical consistency.**  
Consistency of a current body of claims $\neq$ finality of that body. A consistent notebook can be reopened.

**Decision / action.**  
Acting treats some claims as fixed *for this action*. That is use-as-if, not exemption from inquiry. After the action, reassessment remains available.

**Infinite regress.**  
“Must terminate or the regress is contradictory” needs an exhibited contradiction. An open-ended *possibility* of further reassessment is not a completed infinite chain of performed steps. The contradiction appears only if one also requires that inquiry exist as a finished total object. That smuggles closure into the definition of success.

**Definition of “complete.”**  
If “complete” is stipulated to mean “no further possible reassessment,” closure is necessary by wording, not by warrant. This investigation already refuses that stipulation.

**Reproducibility / communicability.**  
Others need a recorded stopping point. That is documentation of $\mathrm{Stop}(I)$ or $\mathrm{Complete}(I)$, not $\mathrm{Final}(I)$.

### 7.2 Countermodel looked for

A methodology that terminates particular investigations indefinitely, without a globally final stopping point.

No incoherence in that pattern was produced by the candidates above. The present repository already *behaves* like that countermodel; OC1 does not treat that behavior as a proof, only as an existence-shaped illustration of the distinction.

### 7.3 Converse: where provisionality would fail

Looked for a task that is incoherent unless a terminal criterion exists.

- Holding-for-action requires provisional fixity, not terminal closure.
- Avoiding explosion requires not affirming contradictions now, not sealing the archive.
- Defining a task as “produce the last word” makes closure necessary by the task-description. That is not an independent warrant.

No non-stipulative failure of provisionality was isolated in this pass.
