## OC4 — Can any purported final criterion survive its own reassessment?

Let $C$ be offered as a final criterion for when inquiry is settled.

Question actually asked:

Does $C$ *establish*

$$
C \;\Rightarrow\; \text{no reassessment of }C\text{ is permitted}
$$

or does its finality come from how the task or frame was drawn?

Distinction kept rigid:

$$
\boxed{\text{decisively sufficient within a frame}}
\qquad\neq\qquad
\boxed{\text{permanently beyond reassessment}}
$$

OC4 tries to produce the second from the first.

### Escape routes

**Logic makes $C$ final.**  
Non-contradiction is decisive *inside* a classical frame. Reassessment of the frame (paraconsistent programs, alternative consequence relations) is an actual form of inquiry. LNC-in-classical-logic is frame-decisive. It was not shown that reassessment of the frame is forbidden by LNC itself.

**The system is complete.**  
Completeness is a property of a specified $S$. It does not say $S$ cannot be left. Gödel-shaped limits, where they apply, cut *against* treating completeness as a general final $C$ for inquiry.

**Otherwise inquiry cannot function.**  
OC1–OC2 already: function required provisional stopping, not an unreassessable $C$. Repeating the function argument does not upgrade $C$ to exemption.

**We defined finality that way.**  
Stipulation. Survives only as wording. Not a warrant that reassessment is illicit.

**Empirical exhaustion.**  
“All cases in this space have been checked” is $\mathrm{Complete}(I)$ given that individuation of cases. New cases, or a new carving of the space, reopen. Exhaustion-in-a-space $\neq$ $C$ beyond reassessment.

**Self-evidence / the given.**  
Historically withdrawn too often to count as “reassessment forbidden.” No independent warrant isolated that the given cannot be asked for its credentials.

**Performative necessity.**  
To inquire, something is held at $t$. That is $\mathrm{Accepted}_t$, not “this $C$ may never be reassessed.”

**Axioms of $S$.**  
Final in $S$. $S$ remains leavable. Same as OC2’s theoremhood case.

**$C$ applied to $C$ and “passes.”**  
Passes only if “pass” means the present inquiry chose not to withdraw $C$. That reports $\mathrm{Accepted}_t(C)$, not exemption.

**The Cut as $C$.**  
$Z(S)$ as a structural condition on particularity is not, by itself, a rule that inquiry into $Z$ or into the demand for closure must stop. Using the Cut as a *terminal criterion* adds a claim OC4 is not allowed to assume ($H_{\mathrm{OC}}$ / “the demand is the Cut”). So the Cut was not permitted to survive as $C$ by inheritance from the framework.

### What would have counted as survival

A $C$ that remains final *under unrestricted reassessment*, without defining reassessment away and without identifying a frame with inquiry.

No tested candidate did that.
