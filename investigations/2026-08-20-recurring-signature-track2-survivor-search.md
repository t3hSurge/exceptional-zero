# Investigation: Recurring Signature — Track 2 (Survivor Search)

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status of investigation:** Open / in progress  
**Related canonical claim:** Recurring Signature (Part Eight) remains **Open**

---

## 1. Question

Does there exist at least one third-person relational account, in any of the three domains (for-this, truth, diachronic identity), that is independently specified, survives the circularity check and the target-shift check, and successfully isolates its target under the framework’s adversarial tests?

## 2. Method constraints

- The candidate relation or property must be stated *before* any appeal to the target (§7.5 anti-circularity rule).
- It must not succeed only by explaining an adjacent phenomenon (target-shift check).
- Thermostat-style or other clearly insufficient substitutes must be blocked.
- Implanted-memory (identity) or privileged-mapping (truth) style counterexamples must be faced directly.

## 3. Candidates tested

### Candidate A — Causal-informational continuity with branching discrimination (Identity)

**Independent specification:**  
A later stage *x* and an earlier stage *y* stand in the relation *C* iff:
1. There is a continuous causal chain from *y* to *x*, and
2. The informational content available at *x* about the chain is such that any alternative chain that would produce identical content at *x* is of measure zero (or is excluded by the actual physical laws governing the system).

**Adversarial pressure:** Implanted-memory and branching cases both survive the two conditions unless further clauses are added that reintroduce the target. Continuity ≠ identity (target-shift).

**Verdict:** Does not survive.

### Candidate B — Primitive correspondence under a fixed interpretation function (Truth)

**Independent specification:**  
A representation *r* is true iff the interpretation function *I* (fixed in advance by the linguistic community or by causal-historical links) maps *r* onto a fact that obtains.

**Adversarial pressure:** Privileged-mapping problem returns for *I* itself. Causal-historical fixing collapses into previously rejected tracking/provenance accounts (overbreadth).

**Verdict:** Does not survive.

### Candidate D — Genuine past as the chain that forces Z on the biographical structure (Identity)

**Independent specification (attempted):**  
Consider the structure S whose elements are candidate biographical stages and whose relations are the ordinary third-person ones (temporal, causal, memory/content). Let the genuine past be the unique subset G ⊆ S such that the induced substructure on G is the minimal substructure for which *Z(G)* holds in a way that is not shared by any rival implant-isomorphic subset.

**Adversarial pressure:**

- The specification already uses “*Z*” and “minimal substructure that forces non-transitivity.” But in the explicit counterfeit model (Track 1), genuine and counterfeit are symmetric; neither forces *Z* relative to the other. Both induce isomorphic substructures. Therefore no unique G satisfies the condition.
- If one adds “the substructure that is actual” or “the one continuous with the present stage’s intrinsic particularity,” the target has been reintroduced.
- Circular risk is high: the candidate tries to use the Cut to recover identity, but the Cut only yields particularity once asymmetry is already present. It cannot create the asymmetry between two currently symmetric candidates.

**Verdict:** Does not survive. It either fails to distinguish in the symmetric case or smuggles the target to break the symmetry.

### Candidate E — Truth as preservation of Z-forcing distinctions (Truth)

**Independent specification (attempted):**  
A representation *r* is true iff there exists a distinction D such that (i) D is preserved by *r*, and (ii) D is a distinction that forces *Z* on the relevant structure of the world (i.e., D is an intrinsically particularizing distinction).

**Adversarial pressure:**

- This inherits the original mapping problem: which distinctions are the Z-forcing ones, and how does *r* latch onto exactly those rather than onto isomorphic but non-actual distinctions?
- It also risks target-shift: preserving a particularizing distinction is closer to “accurately reflecting an asymmetry” than to truth in the ordinary sense. Many true statements do not themselves force new Z; many false statements can preserve existing asymmetries while misrepresenting other facts.
- When pressed, the candidate either becomes a notational variant of “corresponds to an actual particularizing fact” (returning to correspondence difficulties) or fails to cover ordinary truths that do not themselves create or force Exceptional Zeros.

**Verdict:** Does not survive. It does not clear the mapping problem and introduces new coverage failures.

### Candidate C — (for-this domain)

Still no new candidate that clears the prior five failed vocabularies plus the evidence-specification barriers (circularity / target-shift / underdetermination). Any proposal must be independently specified and must not reduce to a thermostat-level architecture or a disguised synonym for “for-this.”

## 4. Result so far

Four candidates examined (A, B, D, E). All fail under adversarial pressure for diagnosable reasons: either underdetermination in the presence of a relational copy, circular reintroduction of the target, collapse into previously rejected accounts, or target-shift. No survivor has been found.

The failures continue to exhibit the recurring signature, but this remains a record of unsuccessful attempts, not a proof that no survivor exists.

## 5. Next steps

- Construct a candidate that deliberately uses only resources already ruled Derived in the framework (the Cut itself, the Actualization Boundary as regulative, etc.) and test whether those resources can be applied non-circularly to one of the three targets.
- Explore whether a purely negative characterization (“the unique chain that is *not* a perfect relational copy of another”) can be made to work without presupposing which one is original.
- Keep generating candidates; a single clear survivor in any domain would immediately show the recurring signature is not exceptionless.

## 6. Provisional status of the meta-claim

**Open.** Track 2 continues. Accumulating well-diagnosed failures tightens confidence that the pattern is robust for ordinary third-person relational resources, but does not yet convert the recurring signature into a Boundary or a Derived consequence of the Cut.
