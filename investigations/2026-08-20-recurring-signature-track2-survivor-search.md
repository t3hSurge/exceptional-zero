# Investigation: Recurring Signature — Track 2 (Survivor Search)

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status of investigation:** Open / in progress  
**Related canonical claim:** Recurring Signature (Part Eight) remains **Open**

---

## 1. Question

Does there exist at least one third-person relational account, in any of the three domains (for-this, truth, diachronic identity), that is independently specified, survives the circularity check and the target-shift check, and successfully isolates its target under the framework’s adversarial tests?

## 2. Method constraints

- The candidate relation or property must be stated *before* any appeal to the target (§7.5 anti-circularity rule).
- It must not succeed only by explaining an adjacent phenomenon (target-shift check).
- Thermostat-style or other clearly insufficient substitutes must be blocked.
- Implanted-memory (identity) or privileged-mapping (truth) style counterexamples must be faced directly.

## 3. First candidates under test

### Candidate A — Causal-informational continuity with branching discrimination (Identity domain)

**Independent specification:**  
A later stage *x* and an earlier stage *y* stand in the relation *C* iff:
1. There is a continuous causal chain from *y* to *x*, and
2. The informational content available at *x* about the chain is such that any alternative chain that would produce identical content at *x* is of measure zero (or is excluded by the actual physical laws governing the system).

**Target it is supposed to recover:** Genuine diachronic identity (the actual past rather than an implanted or branching rival).

**Initial adversarial pressure:**

- **Implanted-memory case:** Suppose a perfect memory implant that copies both content and the apparent causal history markers. Condition 2 appears to fail because the implant is engineered to be informationally indistinguishable. If the implant is physically realized, it is not “measure zero.” The relation either fails to exclude it or must quietly add a further condition (“the chain must be the original biological/physical one”), which reintroduces the target.
- **Branching case (Parfit-style):** Two later stages both stand in continuous causal-informational relations to the same earlier stage. *C* holds for both; identity is not recovered as unique.
- **Target-shift risk:** The relation may succeed at tracking causal-informational continuity while still leaving the “same self” question open. Continuity is not identity.

**Provisional verdict on Candidate A:** Fails the implanted-memory and branching tests without additional clauses that smuggle the target. Does not survive.

### Candidate B — Primitive correspondence under a fixed interpretation function (Truth domain)

**Independent specification:**  
A representation *r* is true iff the interpretation function *I* (fixed in advance by the linguistic community or by causal-historical links) maps *r* onto a fact that obtains.

**Initial adversarial pressure:**

- The privileged-mapping problem reappears: what fixes *I* itself without circularity or infinite regress? If *I* is fixed by further representations, the problem iterates. If *I* is primitive, we have simply relocated primitivism rather than reduced truth.
- Causal-historical fixing of *I* collapses into the already-tested causal-tracking or causal-provenance candidates (overbreadth: shadows, bruises, reliable thermostats).
- The candidate therefore either fails to specify a non-circular *I* or reduces to a previously rejected account.

**Provisional verdict on Candidate B:** Does not survive. It either inherits the mapping problem or collapses into earlier failures.

### Candidate C — (Placeholder for for-this domain)

No new candidate yet formulated that is both independently specified and stronger than the five vocabularies already rejected in Part Four. Any future candidate must clear the thermostat test and the evidence-specification failures (circularity, target-shift, underdetermination) recorded there.

## 4. Result so far

Two initial candidates (one identity, one truth) have been stated independently and subjected to immediate adversarial pressure. Both fail for diagnosable reasons already in the neighborhood of the existing Boundaries. No survivor has been found.

This does not prove no survivor exists. It only records that the first two attempts do not succeed.

## 5. Next steps

- Generate further candidates that deliberately avoid the failure modes of A and B.
- Test whether any “hybrid” (e.g., causal + primitive element) can be stated without the primitive element simply being the target under another name.
- Keep the search domain-open: a single survivor in any one domain would already show that the recurring signature is not exceptionless.

## 6. Provisional status of the meta-claim

**Open.** Track 2 has begun; no status change to the recurring signature is warranted.
