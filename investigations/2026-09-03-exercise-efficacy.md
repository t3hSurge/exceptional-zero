# Exercise and Efficacy

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Complete at this level — AP2 run  
**Depends on:** `2026-09-03-act-upstream-of-actuality.md` (AP1; no upstream act specification earned)  
**Not in scope:** AT or verb-first as lemmas; $Z(S)$; temporal primitives; identity; canonical edit; treating failure as proof of act; generic AB8

## New object

AP1 left a narrow residue: perhaps the missing content of act is not relation, transformation, disposition, or primitive labeling, but **exercise** or **efficacy**.

The question is therefore:

$$
\boxed{\text{Can exercise or efficacy be specified without presupposing actuality, occurrence, or performance?}}
$$

Exercise and efficacy must be kept separate. An efficacy may be a modal or dispositional fact about what would happen under specified conditions; exercise may mean that such a power is actually in operation. The investigation must not assume that distinction is correct, but must test it.

## E1 — Efficacy as modal difference-making

Candidate: define efficacy without actual execution, using counterfactual dependence. Schematically:

$$
\operatorname{Eff}(C,O)
$$

means that under specified intervention/condition $C$, outcome-description $O$ follows across the relevant admissible models.

This can be formulated without an occurrence primitive. It is a relation among conditions, outcomes, and possible models.

That gives an act-free specification of something that may ordinarily be called “efficacy.”

But it does not yet specify **act**. The modal fact says what would obtain under a condition; it does not say that the efficacy is exercised now, nor that an actual production occurs.

Bounded result:

$$
\boxed{\text{Efficacy can be represented act-free as modal difference-making, but that does not constitute exercise.}}
$$

## E2 — Efficacy as actual production

Strengthen efficacy so that it means not merely that $O$ would follow, but that $C$ actually produces $O$.

The candidate now requires an actual production relation. If “produces” is primitive, the actuality/exercise target has simply been stipulated under another name. If production is analyzed through occurrence, performance, realization, or execution, excluded content has returned.

Thus the strengthening does not yield an upstream act specification; it changes efficacy from a modal capacity into an actuality-bearing notion.

$$
\boxed{\text{Actual efficacy does not provide an act-free specification of exercise.}}
$$

## E3 — Exercise as activation of a disposition

Candidate:

$$
\operatorname{Exercise}(P,x)
$$

where $P$ is a power/disposition of $x$ and Exercise says that the disposition is activated or manifested.

The disposition itself can be specified without an actual act. The difficulty is the exercise predicate. “Activated,” “manifested,” and “realized” can mean either:

1. a further modal/structural relation describing what the disposition would do under conditions; or
2. the actual occurrence of the disposition's operation.

On reading (1), exercise collapses back into disposition/efficacy and therefore does not distinguish act from potency.

On reading (2), exercise imports actuality/performance/occurrence.

No third content was isolated.

$$
\boxed{\text{Exercise is either reduced to potency/efficacy or carries the excluded actuality-bearing content.}}
$$

## E4 — Primitive exercise

Allow Exercise as a primitive, irreducible ontological feature.

This remains formally coherent. But the same primitive-content fork from AP1 returns.

If Exercise has no independently discriminating content beyond “this is exercise,” it is a label.

If it has content sufficient to distinguish exercise from unexercised potency, that content must be specified. The tested candidates — activation, realization, manifestation, operation, occurrence, performance — either reduce to the modal/dispositional side or import actuality-bearing content.

So primitive exercise remains a possible stipulation, not an earned upstream account.

## E5 — Efficacy without an actual bearer

Could efficacy itself be fundamental, with no already-actual agent or patient?

A purely formal efficacy relation can be introduced as a primitive relation between abstract relata. But then it faces the same problem as the bare relational act from AP1: a formal relation does not become an exercised efficacy merely by being named so.

If the relata are instead actual agents/patients, actuality has been presupposed. If the relation itself is declared to actualize its relata, actualization has been reintroduced by definition.

No independent route was found.

## Cut

A crucial distinction now survives the pressure:

$$
\boxed{\text{efficacy as modal power}\neq\text{exercise as actual operation}}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{\text{disposition to act}\neq\text{act}}.
$$

This matters because a successful act-free account of efficacy would be a genuine countermodel to any argument that simply identifies act with efficacy. It would not, however, refute the possibility that exercise requires a further actuality-bearing ingredient.

Conversely, failure to specify exercise without actuality does not prove that act is impossible or that actuality is prior. It only leaves the specification problem unresolved.

## AP2 result

Across the tested forms:

$$
\boxed{\text{No tested specification of exercise has supplied content independent of actuality, occurrence, performance, or a collapse into disposition/efficacy.}}
$$

At the same time:

$$
\boxed{\text{Efficacy can be coherently specified as a modal/dispositional relation without act-like content, but it does not thereby constitute an act.}}
$$

This is a useful asymmetry: **efficacy survives as act-free, exercise does not yet.**

## Not earned

- act $\Rightarrow$ actuality;
- actuality $\Rightarrow$ act;
- efficacy $=$ act;
- exercise is impossible without actuality;
- primitive exercise is impossible;
- temporal occurrence is the essence of act;
- structure is a consequence of act.

## Stopping rule

No AP3 is licensed merely by the existence of a residue. A new pressure requires a new independently isolable object.

The present investigation therefore stops here:

$$
\boxed{\text{Modal efficacy is act-free but insufficient for act; exercise remains without an earned non-actuality-dependent specification.}}
$$

The reverse-direction investigation remains open only if a genuinely new object is isolated. The result is a specification boundary, not a metaphysical priority theorem.
