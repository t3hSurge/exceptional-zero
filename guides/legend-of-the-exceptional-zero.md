## 1. Status legend

The framework uses five epistemic statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Derived** | Proven from stated premises or established results. |
| **Believed** | Tested for scope and coherence, but not proven. |
| **Boundary** | A specific search failed in a specific diagnosed way; no universal negative is claimed. |
| **Open** | Unresolved, untested, or inconclusive. |
| **Regulative Principle** | A rule governing permissible inference, not a truth-apt claim. |

> **Nothing in the framework is stronger than its status.**

## 3. The Exceptional Zero

The formal reading aid is:

$$
Z(S)=\neg\mathrm{Transitive}(\mathrm{Aut}(S)).
$$

Read it as:

> The automorphism group of $S$ is not transitive on $S$.

$Z$ is **not** an element, location, region, hidden observer, or causal agent. It is a structural fact about the transformation group.

The central theorem is:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Particularity}(S)\Rightarrow Z(S)}
$$

Here $\mathrm{Particularity}(S)$ means that some element of $S$ is intrinsically singled out by a property expressible from the structure itself.

The converse

$$
Z(S)\Rightarrow\mathrm{Particularity}(S)
$$

is **scope-dependent**. Under a broad invariant-property reading it can hold; under a restricted expressive language it can fail. Do not quote the converse without that qualification.

### The arrow warning

The ordinary arrow $\rightarrow$ is **context-sensitive**. It may represent a directed relation, a conceptual dependency, or an architectural step.

It does **not** automatically mean:

- logical entailment;
- causation;
- temporal succession;
- actual occurrence.

Compare:

$$
A\rightarrow B
$$

with:

$$
A\Rightarrow B.
$$

The first is not, by itself, a proof of the second.

## 5. Joint 3 and Joint 6

### Joint 3 — “I cannot not reach”

Joint 3 is a first-person belief, deliberately scoped to the particular self making the claim.

The framework does **not** derive reaching from finitude:

$$
\text{finitude}\not\Rightarrow\text{reaching}.
$$

The statement is not a universal law governing all finite things.

### Joint 6 — “The world does not want back”

Joint 6 is the corresponding belief about the other side of the relation: the field does not reciprocate the self's reaching.

It does not follow from the Cut alone.

## 6. The Actualization Boundary

The framework's standing inference rule is:

$$
\boxed{\text{possible}\not\Rightarrow\text{actual}}
$$

A structural condition can make something possible without making its occurrence necessary.

This is the **Actualization Boundary** and its status is **Regulative Principle**.

It is a guard against silently moving from what a structure permits to what actually occurs.

## 7. The four levels

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Existence | Something is. |
| **II** | Distinguishability | Something can in principle be told apart. |
| **III** | Intrinsic Particularity | The structure itself privileges one candidate. |
| **IV** | Subjectivity / For-This | There is something it is like *for* that particular to be itself. |

The important boundary is:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{III}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{IV}}
$$

The framework does not derive Level IV from Level III.

For-This remains a **Boundary**: attempted third-person bridges encounter circularity, target-shift, or underdetermination.

## 8. Truth

The framework investigated several candidate reductions of truth, including correspondence, causal tracking, pragmatic success, causal provenance, and distinction-preservation.

No non-circular reductive analysis succeeded.

$$
\boxed{\text{No non-circular reductive analysis of truth has succeeded.}}
$$

This does **not** prove that truth is irreducible.

## 9. Diachronic identity notation

### $O$

**Origin condition** within the Unique Causal Lineage architecture. It is not, by itself, a completed theory of origins.

### $C_*$

The unresolved criterion selecting **which causal features are identity-relevant**.

### $J_{\mathrm{cut}}$

The causal-graph partition obtained once the relevant graph and junction conditions are fixed. It is a **provisional mathematical partition**, not an identity proof.

The identity architecture can be read as:

$$
O
\rightarrow
[\text{identity-relevant causal selection}]
\rightarrow
C_*
\rightarrow
J_{\mathrm{cut}}.
$$

The square brackets mark unresolved machinery.

## 10. Process, material, and common structure

Two causal descriptions are distinguished:

$$
G_P
$$

process-continuity emphasis, and:

$$
G_M
$$

material-provenance emphasis.

The framework does not rank them. The important distinction is:

$$
\boxed{\text{process continuity}\neq\text{material continuity}}
$$

A candidate common structure is represented by:

$$
G^*
$$

with the architectural form:

$$
G_P,G_M
\rightarrow
[\sim]
\rightarrow
[G^*]
\rightarrow
\Sigma.
$$

Here:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\sim$ | candidate representation-equivalence / admissibility relation |
| $[G^*]$ | unresolved common-structure slot |
| $\Sigma$ | unresolved later identity-selection layer |

The first concrete selective candidate was:

$$
G^*_{\mathrm{phys}}=(V,E,\tau).
$$

Its status is:

$$
\boxed{G^*_1\text{ failed as a construction}}
$$

but that does **not** imply:

$$
\boxed{\text{selective }G^*\text{ is impossible}}.
$$
