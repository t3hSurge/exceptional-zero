# AIPC 2026 — candidate essay proposals

These are **proposal-level planning documents, not essay arguments**. The competition protocol permits choosing a topic as a question, but does not permit the human to supply the argument. No proposal below commits the eventual AI-generated essay to a thesis, proof strategy, or conclusion.

Three candidate submissions are being prepared. None is committed yet.

## Proposal A — Symmetry and Particularity

**Working question**

> Can intrinsic particularity be made intelligible in a structure whose automorphisms initially treat its elements as interchangeable?

**Scope**

A philosophy of symmetry, individuation, and structural description. The essay would ask what can legitimately be said when a structure contains distinctions that are not captured by its available symmetries.

**Why it stands alone**

The question is about the relation between symmetry and individuation. It does not require a theory of time, transition, consciousness, or physical ontology.

**Non-goals**

- No imported Exceptional Zero theorem or conclusion.
- No requirement that the essay defend intrinsic particularity.
- No predetermined formal model.

---

## Proposal B — Orientation Without Passage

**Working question**

> What, if anything, distinguishes orientation from transition when a structure contains asymmetric roles but no stipulated temporal or performative process?

**Scope**

A philosophy of relations, direction, change, and structural order. The question asks where the conceptual boundary lies between a relation having non-interchangeable roles and something genuinely occurring or changing.

**Why it stands alone**

The question can be pursued without assuming that time, passage, occurrence, or performance has already been explained. It therefore leaves the central philosophical distinction open rather than building in a process theory.

**Non-goals**

- No imported result that transition fails to reduce to orientation.
- No stipulated definition of transition.
- No assumption that temporal succession is fundamental or derivative.

---

## Proposal C — Totality and Unity

**Working question**

> What makes a totality one thing rather than merely everything?

**Scope**

A philosophy of totality, objecthood, unity, and the possibility of an all-inclusive object. The essay would distinguish exhaustive inclusion from numerical or metaphysical unity and ask what, if anything, could ground the latter.

**Why it stands alone**

The question does not depend on recovering the frozen occurrence line or explaining temporal passage. It can be pursued as a problem about objecthood and unity in its own right.

**Non-goals**

- No assumption that a universal set exists.
- No assumption that totality is numerically one.
- No imported resolution to the unity problem.

## Generation boundary

These proposals deliberately record **questions and scope only**. Once a topic is selected, the essay-generating AI must be given the question without being fed the corresponding arguments, investigation conclusions, or status-table results from the main research program. The full generation protocol is in [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md).

The public repository therefore records the candidate questions while essay bodies and generation logs remain in the private competition repository.
