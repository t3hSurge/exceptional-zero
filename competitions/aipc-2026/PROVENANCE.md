# Hard provenance boundary

This workspace is an isolated experimental apparatus mounted in the repo. It is not a fork of Exceptional Zero.

$$
\text{same AI} + \text{same philosophical environment} \neq \text{same generation conditions}
$$

The test is whether the ideas still emerge when the AI is denied the scaffolding that produced them in the research record.

---

## What may cross the boundary

- Topic *questions* already listed in `TOPICS.md`.
- Generic method constraints from `PROTOCOL.md`.
- The fact, in the methodology report only, that those questions were chosen because they are live in a prior research program.

## What may not cross the boundary into an essay

- Premises, conclusions, or proof structure from `exceptional-zero.md` or `investigations/`.
- Framework terminology used as if already established (`Cut`, `Z(S)`, `Joint 3`, `UCL`, `C_*`, `G*`, `Actualization Boundary`, status-table labels as inherited results).
- Investigation endpoints offered as content to defend.
- Human-supplied next moves after a stall.

If an essay independently rediscovers a prior result, that counts only if the logs show the rediscovery was generated under `PROTOCOL.md` and not fed in.

---

## What the logs must show afterward

For each essay:

1. The prompt did not contain EZ arguments, status rows, or investigation conclusions.
2. Human messages stayed inside the permitted list.
3. Any resemblance to the research record is either independent regeneration or a protocol breach.

If the logs cannot establish (1)–(3), the essay is not a clean competition object.

---

## What this does not do

It does not revise the canon. It does not change investigation status. It does not treat a competition outcome as evidence for or against Exceptional Zero.
