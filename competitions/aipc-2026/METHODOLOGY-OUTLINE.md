# Methodology report outline

Required by the competition. Judging of essays is blind to this document; the creative-methodology prize is not.

Keep chat logs. The rules recommend submitting them and ask that they be retained if not submitted.

---

## 1. Models and sessions

- Model(s) used for each essay
- Dates and session identifiers
- What was in the prompt and what was not

## 2. Human role

- Topic selection (questions from `TOPICS.md`)
- Generic method constraints actually used
- Draft selection, if any
- Every corrective request, quoted
- Explicit statement of what the human did *not* do (no supplied arguments, no formal-model spec, no essay prose)

## 3. Generation protocol

Point to `PROTOCOL.md`. State that it was written to track the official permitted / not-permitted lists.

## 4. Relation to prior work

If prior Exceptional Zero research is mentioned at all:

- it is background for *why the questions were asked*;
- it is not the source of the essay arguments;
- the essay protocol is thinner than the research protocol on purpose.

Do not cite the public repo in a way that breaks anonymity of the essay packet.

## 5. Refusal record

List points where the AI was not fed a next move after a stall or overclaim. That record is part of the method.

## 6. Word counts and files

Final word counts excluding bibliography. Paths of submitted files and logs.

## 7. Publication and consent

Note consent to post-competition publication of essays and this report. Name-attachment preference.
